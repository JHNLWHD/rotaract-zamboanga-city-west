# ADR 0001: Make participation the primary conversion

## Status

Superseded by [ADR 0002: Make proof of legitimacy the primary purpose](0002-legitimacy-as-primary-purpose.md).

The decision and rationale below are historical. Use ADR 0002 for current guidance.

## Decision

The public site will optimize for participation—joining, volunteering, or
forming a community partnership. Projects and events serve as proof of impact
and credibility. Foundation giving remains a secondary conversion path.

## Why

This gives the site one clear job and keeps proof content subordinate to the
action it should support.
