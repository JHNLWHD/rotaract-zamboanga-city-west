# ADR 0002: Make proof of legitimacy the primary purpose

Status: Accepted. Supersedes [ADR 0001](0001-participation-as-primary-conversion.md).

The website must first prove that the club is legitimate, active, and accountable.
Verifiable affiliation, leadership, projects, recognition, Foundation giving,
and contact information take priority over participation. This corrects the
earlier conversion goal, which made club records subordinate to recruitment.

Keep the approved light editorial dossier and Contentful as the record source.
Preserve the chatbot, Get to Know Great West, and View Our Projects. Participation
remains a secondary action, with applications during the intake period and a
notification action outside it. The [home spec](../../openspec/specs/pages/home/spec.md#seasonal-intake)
records the current closed-intake gap; this decision does not implement it or
authorize CMS publication, build-hook changes, or deployment.
